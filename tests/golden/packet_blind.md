# Pairs

A matches B when A's money is spending under the commitment B records. That includes a commitment the donor reported on several lines for the same recipient (same loan or grant, same commitment date), renumbered, or left partly undisbursed and drawn down later; a regional commitment line counts for a country inside that region. It excludes a line for a different recipient country, a sibling project, a later phase or module with its own new commitment even under a reused number, a separate yearly grant or contribution committed and spent in its own year, and an unrelated activity.

Each section shows two aid records from the OECD CRS of one donor. Record A is a later disbursement; record B is an earlier commitment. Amounts are in US dollars at current prices, summed over the CRS rows that share the record's keys, and every such row is listed below the table (up to 12 a side); the texts in the table are those of the largest row. Similar commitments counts the donor's other commitment records for the same recipient, B aside, whose titles share at least half their words with the side's title: siblings, phases and yearly grants to tell apart from B.
