# climate-finance 2.0.0

Version 2.0 moves the package from local matching to versioned releases. Each release holds climate commitments from the OECD's CRDF, the CRS disbursements traced to them, and the evidence for each link. `cf.load()` opens a release, and `Snapshot.spending()` answers a query with a low and a high bound. [Migrating from 1.x](../migrating-from-1x.md) maps each 1.x call to its replacement.

Totals from 2.0 differ from 1.2.3 and from the OECD's own climate totals. The figures on this page come from a build of CRS version v20261006 and CRDF commitment years 2015 to 2024 with the settings `build.toml` ships, which leave the fill stage off (`gap_fill = false`).

## Why totals differ from 1.x

1.x matched each CRDF row to CRS rows on a concatenated key of provider, agency, project id, title, recipient, finance type, modality, purpose, and year, trying five keys in turn and dropping fields from the later ones. Each key that matched ran again without the year, so a later disbursement counted where it carried the same ids as the matched record. 2.0 links a disbursement to its commitment in any year, including under ids the donor changed, allocates it, and reports what remained unlinked.

| Cause | Effect on the total |
|---|---|
| A disbursement counts against its commitment in later years, including under ids the donor changed. | USD 179.1bn of climate disbursements in 2015 to 2024 count against a commitment from an earlier year, USD 6.0bn of it through ids the donor changed. |
| `low` counts only certain links reached by certain routes, and `value` counts every link. 1.x had one figure. | Under ONE, disbursements in 2015 to 2024 total `low` USD 259.1bn and `value` USD 313.0bn. |
| A commitment republished in a later CRDF vintage counts once, and its disbursements count on the first row. | USD 2.7bn of climate commitments in 2015 to 2024 repeat an earlier vintage. |
| An aggregate CRDF row is rebuilt from the CRS records it pools, and the parts count in its place. | USD 8.1bn of climate commitments in 2015 to 2024 are parts rebuilt from aggregates. |
| EIB and GEF commitments that no key reaches link through project lists and crosswalks. With the fill stage on, the Green Climate Fund's and GEF's disbursements also enter from their IATI files, and bank ledgers fill commitments the CRS records no disbursement for. | USD 13.6bn of climate commitments in 2015 to 2024 link through an EIB route (USD 12.9bn) or the GEF crosswalk (USD 0.7bn). |
| Core contributions to multilateral agencies count once, in the provider that pays them, times the agency's climate share. EU budget, EDF, and EIB money counts in its members' totals. | Under ONE, disbursements in 2015 to 2024 total USD 235.3bn in the provider view and USD 313.0bn in the recipient view. |

Other changes move smaller amounts:

- Agency shares for ONE's attribution rest on the agency's climate commitments over its CRS commitments, summed over the share year and the year before. 1.x divided matched climate disbursements by all CRS disbursements, and read zero wherever matching failed.
- A CRDF row with a negative face value, a published downward revision, stays in `commitments` as published and has nothing disbursed.
- The CRS writes "Developing countries, unspecified" as 9998 and the CRDF as 998. Every release table uses 998.
- Private-sector and philanthropic flows (Bi_Multi 6) stay out of the views.
- CRDF rows carry no Bi_Multi code. The build sets it to the provider's most common Bi_Multi among its non-core CRS records, and to 2 for imputed multilateral contributions.

## ONE's 2024 headline figures

ONE published two headline figures in African Arguments in January 2024: "between 2013 and 2021, $228 billion in climate finance commitments had not been disbursed", and for "an additional $69 billion" ONE "couldn't even find disbursements data". Neither figure had a recorded method, so the difference from 2.0 cannot be split into causes line by line.

The 2.0 trace restates both under ONE for 2015 to 2021, the commitment years the release holds within 2013 to 2021.

| Status | Climate commitments under ONE, USD bn |
|---|---|
| `disbursed` (at least 90% of face value allocated) | 136.1 |
| `partly_disbursed` | 182.4 |
| `no_disbursement_record` | 41.1 |
| `not_traceable` | 62.4 |
| `pending_review` | 0.0 |

The USD 228bn figure becomes the undisbursed climate value of commitments with a disbursement record, the climate value of the `disbursed` and `partly_disbursed` commitments less the climate value of what the trace allocated to them, which comes to USD 105.4bn. The USD 69bn figure becomes the climate value of commitments with no disbursement found, the `no_disbursement_record` and `not_traceable` rows above, which stay separate. A commitment is `not_traceable` when it reaches no CRS commitment or its agency files neither a crs id nor a project number that persists from year to year. That reflects a gap in donor reporting, so the package records it apart from non-payment.

## How sure the links are

A blind sample measures each provider's link precision. The release gate requires the lower bound of each provider's interval to reach 0.90, and people decide at least one draw in ten.

For any gated release, `snap.quality().precision` reports each provider's estimate, its interval, and whether it passed the gate.

## Licences

The Green Climate Fund's projects API and readiness list bar redistribution and derivative works without written consent. Their disbursement rows are withheld from the release. `commitments.withheld_usd` states what they disbursed to each commitment, which the commitment's `disbursed_usd` and the `quality` table include and `spending()` figures leave out. Withheld rows come from the fill stage, so a build with `gap_fill = false` withholds nothing. The Green Climate Fund's IATI files are CC0 on the IATI Registry, and their rows ship. `sources.json` in each pack lists every source with its licence and a note on rows left out. [How linking works](../explanation/how-linking-works.md) and [Release tables](../reference/release-tables.md) have the detail.

## New in 2.0

- `cf.load()`, `Snapshot.spending()`, `quality()`, `reconcile()`, and `disbursement_profile()`. [Python API](../reference/python-api.md) describes them.
- `cf.methods.ONE`, `OECD`, `PROVIDER`, and `RANGE`, and `Method` for your own coefficients, with `relevance="jev"` to read Jev's adaptation levels.
- `result.rows()`, `result.explain()`, `result.share_of_total()`, and `Spec` for tracing and rerunning a figure. `cf.compare()` sets results side by side.
- `snap.reconcile()`, which sets a party's BTR total against OECD figures.
- `cf-build`, which builds, reviews, gates, and packs releases. [cf-build](../reference/cf-build.md) lists its commands.

## Removed or changed

`ClimateData` runs with a `DeprecationWarning` until 3.0. `set_climate_finance_data_path` and `get_unfccc_data` import with the same warning and raise `NotImplementedError` naming their replacement when called. Net disbursements have no 2.0 equivalent. The other removed calls raise an error that names their replacement. The package requires Python 3.12 or later.
