# climate-finance 2.0.0

Version 2.0 moves the package from local matching to versioned releases. Each release holds climate commitments from the OECD's CRDF, the CRS disbursements traced to them, and the evidence for each link. `cf.load()` opens a release, and `Snapshot.spending()` answers a query with a low and a high bound. [Migrating from 1.x](../migrating-from-1x.md) maps each 1.x call to its replacement.

Totals from 2.0 differ from 1.2.3 and from the OECD's own climate totals.

## Why totals differ from 1.x

1.x matched each CRDF row to CRS rows on a concatenated key that included the commitment year, trying five keys in turn, and counted what matched. 2.0 links a disbursement to its commitment in any year, allocates it, and reports what remained unlinked.

| Cause | Effect on the total |
|---|---|
| A disbursement counts against its commitment in later years. 1.x keyed on the year, so a later-year disbursement missed the match. | {{figure: climate disbursements 2015 to 2024 added by multi-year tracing, USD bn}} |
| Estimated links count in `value` and stay out of `low`. 1.x had one figure. | `low` {{figure: low-bound total 2015 to 2024 under ONE, USD bn}}, `value` {{figure: value total 2015 to 2024 under ONE, USD bn}} |
| A commitment republished in a later CRDF vintage counts once, and its disbursements count on the first row. | {{figure: USD bn of commitments counted twice across vintages, 2015 to 2024}} |
| An aggregate CRDF row is rebuilt from the CRS records it pools, and the parts count in its place. | {{figure: USD bn of commitments rebuilt from aggregates, 2015 to 2024}} |
| EIB, GCF, and GEF commitments that no key reaches link through project lists, crosswalks, and the funds' IATI files. | {{figure: USD bn of commitments traced by recovery routes, 2015 to 2024}} |
| Core contributions to multilateral agencies count once, in the provider that pays them, times the agency's climate share. EU budget, EDF, and EIB money counts in its members' totals. | {{figure: provider-view total 2015 to 2024 under ONE, USD bn}} against {{figure: recipient-view total for the same years, USD bn}} |

Other changes move smaller amounts:

- Agency shares for ONE's attribution rest on the agency's climate commitments over its CRS commitments, summed over the share year and the year before. 1.x divided matched climate disbursements by all CRS disbursements, and read zero wherever matching failed.
- A CRDF row with a negative face value, a published downward revision, stays in `commitments` as published and has nothing disbursed.
- The CRS writes "Developing countries, unspecified" as 9998 and the CRDF as 998. Every release table uses 998.
- Private-sector and philanthropic flows (Bi_Multi 6) stay out of the views.
- CRDF rows carry no Bi_Multi code. The build derives it from the provider's class in the CRS, and sets 2 for imputed multilateral contributions.

## Licences

The Green Climate Fund's projects API and readiness list bar redistribution and derivative works without written consent. Their disbursement rows are withheld from the release. `commitments.withheld_usd` states what they disbursed to each commitment, which the commitment's `disbursed_usd` and the `quality` table include and `spending()` figures leave out. For 2015 to 2024 the withheld amount is {{figure: withheld_usd total, USD bn}}. The Green Climate Fund's IATI files are CC0 on the IATI Registry, and their rows ship. `sources.json` in each pack lists every source with its licence and a note on rows left out. [How linking works](../explanation/how-linking-works.md) and [Release tables](../reference/release-tables.md) have the detail.

## New in 2.0

- `cf.load()`, `Snapshot.spending()`, `quality()`, `reconcile()`, and `disbursement_profile()`. [Python API](../reference/python-api.md) describes them.
- `cf.methods.ONE`, `OECD`, `PROVIDER`, and `RANGE`, and `Method` for your own coefficients, with `relevance="jev"` to read Jev's adaptation levels.
- `result.rows()`, `result.explain()`, `result.share_of_total()`, and `Spec` for tracing and rerunning a figure. `cf.compare()` sets results side by side.
- `snap.reconcile()`, which sets a party's BTR total against OECD figures.
- `cf-build`, which builds, reviews, gates, and packs releases. [cf-build](../reference/cf-build.md) lists its commands.

## Removed or changed

`ClimateData`, `set_climate_finance_data_path`, and `get_unfccc_data` import with a `DeprecationWarning` until 3.0. Net disbursements have no 2.0 equivalent. The other removed calls raise an error that names their replacement. The package requires Python 3.12 or later.
